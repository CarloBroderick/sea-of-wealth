About the author

Ben Halpern

Marine biologist, conservation scientist, and one of the world’s leading thinkers on the relationship between people and the ocean.

Ben Halpern is professor of marine biology and conservation science at the University of California, Santa Barbara, and executive director of the National Center for Ecological Analysis and Synthesis.

His research has appeared in Nature, Science, and Scientific American, and his work is regularly featured by NPR, the BBC, and other major media outlets.
