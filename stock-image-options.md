# Ocean photography options

Carlo's direction (2026-11-03): every image on the site should be a true top-down (nadir) aerial photograph of the ocean and activity on it — no oblique angles, no horizons, no beach/resort shots.

## Images used in this prototype (all top-down, Unsplash free license)

1. **Fishing-net pattern — hero**
   Abstract top-down view of a fishing-net array on open water. 2200×1650 (source up to 8064×6048).
   https://unsplash.com/photos/a-large-group-of-fishing-nets-in-the-water-yVBK96fIRlc

2. **Circular fish farms — Food**
   Direct top-down view of aquaculture rings, closely echoing the book cover. 2200×1237 (source up to 8064×4536).
   https://unsplash.com/photos/circular-fish-farms-in-the-ocean-PM-ONyGpkXM

3. **Container ship — Transport**
   Full top-down view of a cargo ship underway. 2200×1585 (source up to 4022×2897).
   https://unsplash.com/photos/an-aerial-view-of-a-cargo-ship-in-the-ocean-0A7YwYhZhWw

4. **Offshore construction vessel — Energy**
   Top-down view of a vessel used for offshore energy infrastructure work. 2200×1237 (source up to 4032×2268).
   https://unsplash.com/photos/aerial-view-of-a-large-offshore-construction-vessel-lIV47M91fd8

5. **Seaweed farm with two workers — Carbon**
   Top-down view of a seaweed farm and the people tending it; represents ocean-based carbon and food solutions. 2200×3911 (source up to 2250×4000).
   https://unsplash.com/photos/two-people-in-a-boat-on-a-seaweed-farm-5ld5u8W9n0k

6. **Small boat with a net — full-width transition / immersive section**
   Top-down view of a single working boat, used for the human-scale quote section. 2200×1650 (source up to 3409×2557).
   https://unsplash.com/photos/an-aerial-view-of-a-boat-in-the-ocean-twN602hr4uM

All six are Unsplash Free photographs, usable commercially under the [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license); credit is appreciated, not required. Packaged derivatives are WebP, resized and compressed for the web.
